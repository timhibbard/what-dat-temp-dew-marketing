Privacy Policy

What Dat Temp Dew · Last updated November 23, 2026

The short version

The app doesn't have accounts, and we don't run any servers that collect your data. Your location is used to look up the weather and is never sold or shared with advertisers. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which may collect device data as described below. Any tip turns ads off.

Location

With your permission ("While Using the App"), the app uses your location to get weather for where you are. If you save a home location, its coordinates and name are stored only on your device so the widgets and alerts can use them. Your location is sent to Apple's WeatherKit service to get the forecast and is not passed to the ad SDK or anyone else. The app never asks for "Always" location access.

Weather data

Forecasts come from Apple WeatherKit. Apple's handling of these requests is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Your settings

Comfort thresholds, notification preferences, quiet hours, mute status and cached weather are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Notifications

Alerts are created on your device from the forecast. No push notification server is involved, and no notification data leaves your device.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

If you haven't tipped, the app shows a banner ad from Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers (including the advertising identifier, if you allow tracking), IP address, and ad interaction data to show and measure ads.

  • The app asks for permission to track (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, Google shows ads that aren't personalized.
  • In the EU, UK and Switzerland, you'll see a consent form first. You can change your choice any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • See how Google uses data in ads and Google's Privacy Policy.

Tips and purchases

Tips are handled by Apple through the App Store. We don't receive your payment details. The app only checks with Apple whether you've tipped so it can turn ads off.

Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
