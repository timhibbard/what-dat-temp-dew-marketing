Should you open the window?

What Dat Temp Dew checks the temperature, humidity and dew point outside, now and for the next three hours, and gives you a straight answer.

Open the window68° · 52% humidity, and it stays that way for the next 3 hours.
Open now, close by 4 PMHumidity climbs past 60% around 4.
Keep it closedToo humid right now. Opens around 7 PM.

What it does

  • A clear verdict, using your own comfort range (60–72°F and humidity under 60% by default).
  • Current conditions plus the next three hours, with the reading that changes the answer highlighted.
  • A heads-up before it's time to close: "Open now, close by 4 PM".
  • Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, a Control Center control, and Siri: "What dat temp?"
  • Optional alerts when it's a good time to open or close, with quiet hours and a 24-hour mute.
  • Uses your current location or a saved home.
