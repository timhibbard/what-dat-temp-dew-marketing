How does the app decide?

It recommends opening the window when the temperature is in your comfort range (60–72°F by default) and humidity is at or below your limit (60% by default). It checks the next three hours too, so it can tell you when to close it. You can change all three values in Settings.

Why does the widget show my home and not where I am?

Widgets and alerts always use your saved home, because that's where your windows are. The app itself can switch between Home and your current location.

My alerts came a little late.

iOS decides when apps can refresh in the background, so alert timing is approximate. The "close soon" alert is scheduled ahead of time from the forecast to help with this.

How do I pause alerts?

Long-press any alert and tap Mute for 24 Hours, tap the bell in the app, or say "Mute Dew Check alerts" to Siri. You can also set quiet hours in Settings → Alerts.

What can I ask Siri?

"What dat temp in Dew Check?" tells you whether to open the window at home, with the temperature and humidity. "Mute Dew Check alerts" pauses alerts for 24 hours. Both also work in the Shortcuts app.

What's the "Later" row?

On iPad and an unfolded iPhone Duo, Dew Check also shows the 12 hours after the next three, each marked ✓ when it's good for an open window, so you can see when the next good stretch starts. The answer at the top still uses the next three hours.

I tipped. How do I get rid of ads on a new phone?

Open Settings → Tip Jar → Restore Purchases.

Why °C instead of °F (or the other way around)?

The app follows your iPhone's region setting: Settings → General → Language & Region → Temperature.
